Partner with us

Join us before the first pilot.

Baraare is new, and honest about it. We bring local delivery and measurement discipline; we are looking for partners who value both.

What we seek

Four kinds of help.

  • Consortium & sub-grantee roles

    Especially in water: we join experienced consortia as a local delivery and accountability partner.

  • Fiscal sponsorship

    A registered sponsor to receive and account for pilot funding while our own registration completes.

  • Diaspora & community support

    Somali professionals at home and abroad: review our materials, mentor young facilitators, open doors.

  • In-kind help

    Training venues, printing, transport, technical review — the practical things that move a pilot.

What we offer

Three things we’re good at.

  • Local delivery

    A Mogadishu-based team working in Somali, on the ground in Banadir, with community relationships that outlast projects.

  • Data capability

    Founded by a statistician and price analyst: clean baselines, honest denominators, results you can audit.

  • Measurement discipline

    Baseline before delivery. A public headline metric. Failures published, not buried.

How money moves

No donate button. On purpose.

We do not take online payments or donations, and this site will not ask for them. Support flows through written agreements, dual-signatory accounts and — where a funder requires it — a fiscal sponsor.

If you want to fund the pilot, write to us. We will share the plan, the budget and the safeguards, and agree the proper route together.

If anyone solicits funds in Baraare’s name, it is not us — please report it to info@baraareinitiative.org.
